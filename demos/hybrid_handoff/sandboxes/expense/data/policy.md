# Expense Policy

## meals
Business meals are reimbursable. Per-person cap is **$75** including tax and tip.
Group meals are allowed but must stay under the per-person cap when divided by the
number of attendees. Solo meals over $40 require a note. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## travel
Ground transport (taxi, rideshare) is reimbursable with no fixed cap. Flights must
be booked in economy; business class requires VP pre-approval.

## lodging
Hotel stays are reimbursable up to **$400/night**. Anything above requires manager
approval and a justification.

## equipment
Personal equipment purchases up to **$150** can be expensed without approval. Above
that, file an IT asset request instead.

## general
Submit within 30 days. Receipts are required for any expense over $25.
